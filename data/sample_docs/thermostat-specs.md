# Helios Thermostat X2 — Specifications

*Last updated: February 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## Hardware

- Display: 3.5" color touchscreen
- Temperature range: 40°F to 95°F (5°C to 35°C)
- Sensors: temperature, humidity, occupancy, ambient light
- Battery: rechargeable lithium-ion, **up to 18 months** per charge in typical use
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n (2.4 GHz only), Bluetooth 5.0 for setup

## Power

The X2 charges over its C-wire. Homes without a C-wire can use the included
**power adapter kit** — installation takes about 10 minutes and no electrician
is required.

## In the box

Thermostat X2, trim plate, mounting screws, wire labels, power adapter kit,
quick-start guide.
