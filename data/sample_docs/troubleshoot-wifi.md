# Helios Home — Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

*Last updated: March 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## Hub won't connect to Wi-Fi

1. Confirm your network is **2.4 GHz**. Helios devices do not support 5 GHz-only
   networks. Most routers let you enable a separate 2.4 GHz SSID.
2. Move the hub within 15 feet of the router during setup.
3. Restart the hub: hold the side button for 10 seconds until the light turns amber,
   then release.
4. In the Helios app, go to Settings → Devices → Reconnect and follow the prompts.

## Thermostat shows "offline"

- Check that the hub's light is solid green (online).
- If the hub is online but the thermostat is offline, press the thermostat's crown
  button once to wake it, then wait 2 minutes.
- As a last resort, remove the thermostat from its wall plate for 30 seconds to
  force a reboot. Your schedules are preserved.

## Still stuck?

Run the in-app **Connection Diagnostic** (Settings → Support → Diagnostics). It
tests each hop — device to hub, hub to router, router to cloud — and tells you
exactly which link failed.
