# Helios Home — Returns Policy

*Last updated: March 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## 30-day returns

You may return any unused device in its original packaging within **30 days** of
delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is free — we email you a prepaid label.

## After 30 days

Returns between day 31 and day 60 are accepted with a **10% restocking fee**.
After 60 days, all sales are final (the 2-year warranty still applies).

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** of
the warehouse receiving your return.

## Exchanges

Defective-on-arrival units are exchanged (not refunded) within the first 14 days,
with expedited 2-day shipping at no charge.
