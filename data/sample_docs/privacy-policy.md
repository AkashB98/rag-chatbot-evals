# Helios Home — Privacy Policy (summary)

*Last updated: January 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## What we collect

- Device telemetry: temperature readings, device status, and error logs.
- Account data: name, email, and shipping address.
- App usage: which screens you visit, to improve the product.

## What we never do

- We **never sell your personal data** to third parties or data brokers.
- We never use camera or microphone data for advertising. (Helios devices have no
  cameras; only the Video Doorbell X1 has a camera, and its footage stays local
  unless you enable cloud clips.)

## Retention

Telemetry is kept for **13 months**, then automatically deleted. Account data is
kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account closure.

## Your controls

You can download or delete all your data at any time from the app under
Settings → Privacy → My Data. Deletion completes within 72 hours.
