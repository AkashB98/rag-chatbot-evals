# Helios Home — Shipping Information

*Last updated: March 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## Shipping options

- **Standard (free on orders over $50):** 3–5 business days.
- **Standard (orders under $50):** $4.95, 3–5 business days.
- **Expedited:** $12.95, 2 business days.
- **Overnight:** $24.95, next business day (order by 2 PM CT).

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states. We do not currently ship internationally.

## Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed within 24 hours of your order shipping. Most orders
leave the warehouse within 1 business day.

## Damaged in transit

Report damage within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the box and the device.
We ship a replacement free and file the carrier claim ourselves.
