# Helios Care — Subscription Plans

*Last updated: April 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

Helios Care is our optional subscription for extended coverage and premium features.
All plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime.

## Basic — Free

- Standard 2-year warranty
- Community support forum
- 7 days of energy-history data

## Plus — $9/month

- Everything in Basic
- Extended 4-year warranty
- Priority chat support (under 5-minute response)
- 2 years of energy-history data
- One free device replacement per year for accidental damage

## Pro — $19/month

- Everything in Plus
- Lifetime warranty for as long as you subscribe
- Phone support, 7 days a week
- Unlimited energy-history data
- Annual professional energy audit (a $149 value)
