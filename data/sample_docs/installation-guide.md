# Helios Thermostat X2 — Installation Guide

*Last updated: February 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## Before you start

Turn off power to your HVAC system at the breaker. Installation takes most people
**20–30 minutes** and requires only a screwdriver.

## Do you need a C-wire?

- If your old thermostat has a wire connected to the **C terminal**, you have a
  C-wire — connect it to the X2's C terminal and skip the adapter.
- **No C-wire?** Use the included power adapter kit. It draws power from your
  furnace's control board through two labeled wires — no electrician needed, and
  it is covered by the standard warranty.

## Steps

1. Label each wire on your old thermostat with the included stickers.
2. Mount the trim plate and X2 base to the wall.
3. Connect wires to the matching labeled terminals (R, W, Y, G, C).
4. Snap on the display, restore power, and follow the on-screen setup.
5. The X2 runs a 5-minute system test, then joins your Wi-Fi.

## Compatibility

Works with 24V forced-air, heat pump (with or without aux heat), and radiant
systems. **Not compatible** with line-voltage (120/240V) baseboard heaters.
