# Helios Home — Warranty Policy

*Last updated: March 2026. This document is fictional sample data for demo purposes.*

## Coverage

Every Helios Home device ships with a **2-year limited warranty** starting on the
delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use.

## What is covered

- Faulty sensors, displays, and internal electronics
- Battery failures within the first 12 months
- Hub connectivity hardware defects

## What is NOT covered

- Damage from drops, water exposure, or power surges
- Normal wear and tear, including cosmetic scratches
- Devices purchased from unauthorized resellers
- Problems caused by third-party firmware or modifications

## How to file a claim

1. Contact support with your order number and a photo of the issue.
2. Support approves or denies the claim within 3 business days.
3. Approved claims: a replacement ships free within 5 business days. You do not
   need to return the defective unit for items under $75.
